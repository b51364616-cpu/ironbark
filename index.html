You do not have to service at the dealer.

It's the single most common thing we get asked, and the answer has been settled law in Australia since 2010. Under the Competition and Consumer Act, a manufacturer cannot void your new car warranty simply because an independent workshop did the servicing.

What matters is that the work follows the manufacturer's schedule, uses parts of the right specification, and is properly documented. That's exactly what we do — and we're usually 30 to 40 per cent cheaper than the dealership for the same service.

We keep the receipts, the part numbers and the technician's name on file for seven years. If a manufacturer ever asks, we answer.Dean Whitlock, owner
